# Gaming Hub Setup Guide

> This is a sample guide. Replace it with the real steps for your template.

## 1. Add your bots

1. Invite **Dyno** and set up an **Auto Role** for new members.
2. Invite any other bots you want, then add each one to the **Bots** role.

## 2. Rename the game channels

Rename the channels under **Play** to match the games your community actually plays.

## 3. Review roles

Open **Server Settings → Roles** and assign staff roles to your team.
